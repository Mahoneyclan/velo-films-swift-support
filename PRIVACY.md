# Velo Films privacy policy

Effective date: November 2026

**The short version:** Velo Films works on your own footage, on your own device and drives. The developer runs no server and collects nothing. The app connects to Strava, only when you sign in or import a ride, and to Apple Maps for the maps in your videos.

## What data the app uses

- **Camera footage and the videos it makes:** read from and written to the folders or drives you choose. They never leave your device or drives.
- **Ride GPS tracks:** imported from Strava or from a `.gpx` file you add, and saved in your project folder. A GPS track can show where a ride starts and ends, such as near your home, so keep project folders somewhere private.
- **Strava sign-in tokens:** stored encrypted in the Keychain, on this device only. On iPhone and iPad they are not synced to iCloud or restored to other devices.
- **Folder permissions:** on iPhone and iPad, the app remembers only the folders you picked in Settings.
- **Your own music**, if you add it: kept on your device for use in your videos.
- **App settings.**

The app does not access your location, contacts, camera, microphone or photo library.

## Where data goes

The app connects only to:

- **Strava** (Strava, Inc., USA): to sign in to your Strava account and import your rides and their GPS tracks, when you ask it to. You can revoke Velo Films's access at any time at strava.com/settings/apps.
- **Apple Maps:** to draw the map shown in your videos. Apple receives the map area being drawn, as with any Apple Maps request.

All connections use HTTPS. Velo Films does not:

- sell, share or send your data to anyone else
- use analytics, advertising or tracking
- upload your footage or videos anywhere

Your use of Strava is covered by Strava's Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.

## Deleting your data

- Your footage, GPS tracks and videos live in the folders you chose: delete them there.
- Signing out of Strava removes the app's Strava tokens. You can also revoke access at strava.com/settings/apps.
- Deleting the app removes its settings and any music stored inside it. iOS may keep the encrypted Strava sign-in in the Keychain after the app is deleted, so a reinstall may still be signed in.

## Children's privacy

Velo Films is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect any information from children.
