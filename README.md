# Velo Films support

## About the app

Velo Films turns your Cycliq bike-camera footage (Fly12 front and Fly6 rear) into a highlight reel of your ride, on iPhone and iPad.

- Matches your clips to the ride's GPS route from Strava or a GPX file.
- Picks the best moments automatically, using speed, gradient and what's happening on camera.
- Renders the reel with a map, speed gauge and elevation overlay, and lets you fine-tune the picks.
- Your footage stays on your device and drives; nothing is uploaded.
